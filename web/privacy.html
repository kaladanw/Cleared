Privacy policy

Your screenshots are personal.

Cleared processes the listing screenshots and optional context you submit to produce a second-opinion report.

Last updated: July 11, 2026

What Cleared processes

When you run a check, Cleared processes the screenshots you choose, any context you enter, technical request data needed to operate and protect the service, and the resulting analysis. Screenshots can contain seller names, profile images, messages, or other details visible on screen. Crop or redact anything you do not want analyzed.

How information is used

Submitted content is used to provide the requested check, maintain service reliability and security, and troubleshoot problems. Cleared does not use your screenshots to advertise to you or sell personal information.

Service providers

Cleared relies on hosting, database, authentication, and AI service providers to operate. Submitted content may be sent to those providers only as needed to deliver the service, subject to their processing terms and configured retention controls.

Storage and deletion

During the beta, reports and associated account information may be retained so you can revisit checks and so Cleared can diagnose failures. Screenshot retention should be minimized and documented before public launch. To request access or deletion, use the support page.

Launch requirement

Before accepting public users, replace this paragraph with the final production retention period and verify the policies of every configured processor.

Your choices

You control which screenshots you submit and what optional context you provide. Do not submit sensitive personal information or content you do not have permission to share.

Children

Cleared is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may change as the beta evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent through Cleared support.
