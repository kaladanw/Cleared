Let’s sort it out.

Cleared is an early beta. If a check fails or the analysis misses something important, include the details below so the issue can be reproduced.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm you have a stable connection.
  2. Try again with clear screenshots that show the listing price, description, and product photos.
  3. Remove unrelated or sensitive content from the screenshots.
  4. Note the time of the check and the app version shown in Settings.

What to include

Describe what happened, what you expected, your iPhone model and iOS version, and any error message. Do not email unredacted screenshots containing private messages, addresses, payment details, or other sensitive information.
