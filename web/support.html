Support

Let’s sort it out.

Cleared is an early beta. If account access, extension setup, or a listing check fails, include the details below so the issue can be reproduced.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm you used the email address that received the invite.
  2. In chrome://extensions, confirm Developer mode is on and Cleared is enabled.
  3. Reload the Depop listing after loading or updating the extension.
  4. Note the time of the problem and copy the exact error message.

What to include

Describe what happened, what you expected, your Chrome version, and any error message. Do not email passwords, account tokens, private messages, addresses, payment details, or other sensitive information.
